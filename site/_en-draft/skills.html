Technical expertise

Skills linked to concrete use cases

My aim is to produce usable results, with a clear method and deliverables your teams can reuse.

Languages

  • Python: automate file cleaning and produce reproducible analyses.
  • R: explore data and build documented statistical analyses.
  • SQL: extract and consolidate useful data from a relational database.

Visualisation

  • Tableau: create a dashboard to track sales by channel.
  • Power BI: centralise commercial and operational indicators.
  • Looker: share consistent metrics across teams.

Data manipulation

  • Pandas: clean, transform and aggregate a transaction history.
  • Advanced Excel: make existing reporting reliable with formulae and checks.
  • ETL: organise the import of heterogeneous sources into a documented flow.

Statistics and modelling

  • Descriptive statistics: identify trends and anomalies in an activity.
  • Statistical tests: assess how robust a difference between two segments is.
  • Predictive modelling: estimate a cancellation risk from historical data.

Cross-cutting skills

  • Communication: present results to decision-makers and business teams.
  • Plain language: explain a method and its limitations without unnecessary jargon.
  • Project management: frame the stages, deliverables and priorities of an engagement.
