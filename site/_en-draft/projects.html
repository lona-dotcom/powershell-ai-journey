E-commerce · SME

E-commerce dashboard: real-time sales steering

Challenge

Sales data was spread across several sources. The team tracked indicators with a delay and lacked visibility of the best-performing products and channels.

Solution

Data consolidation with SQL, preparation of indicators with Python and creation of a Tableau dashboard with filters by period, channel, product and geographic area.

Results

  • 30% reduction in reporting timescales.
  • 15% recoverable margin on underperforming product lines.
  • A shared view of sales for commercial teams.

Tools and technologies

  • SQL
  • Python
  • Tableau
  • Excel

SaaS · Growing company

Churn analysis: identifying customers at risk

Challenge

The cancellation rate was rising without a clearly identified cause. The company wanted to detect weak signals before customers left.

Solution

Exploratory analysis with Python, customer segmentation by product usage, tenure and support interactions, then construction of an interpretable risk score.

Results

  • 22% of at-risk accounts identified before renewal.
  • 12% reduction in targeted churn after retention actions were activated.
  • A prioritised action plan for the customer success team.

Tools and technologies

  • Python
  • pandas
  • SQL
  • Jupyter

B2B services · Customer database

Cleaning and making a 50,000-row customer database reliable

Challenge

The customer database contained duplicates, inconsistent formats and incomplete information. Teams could not measure commercial performance reliably.

Solution

Profiling of the database, field normalisation, duplicate detection with Python and creation of documented control rules for future imports.

Results

  • 18% reduction in duplicates detected.
  • 96% of records made usable for reporting.
  • A reproducible quality-control process.

Tools and technologies

  • Python
  • pandas
  • SQL
  • Excel

Marketing · Digital brand

Measuring the performance of acquisition campaigns

Challenge

Advertising investment was tracked by channel, but the company had no consolidated view of acquisition cost and the real contribution of each campaign.

Solution

Unification of advertising and commercial data, definition of shared indicators, then creation of automated reporting with SQL and Tableau.

Results

  • 25% of time saved on weekly reporting.
  • 14% of budget reallocated to the most profitable campaigns.
  • A consistent reading of the acquisition journey.

Tools and technologies

  • SQL
  • Tableau
  • Google Analytics
  • Python
