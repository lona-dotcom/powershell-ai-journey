FAQ

Frequently asked questions about freelance data analysts

A clear and practical FAQ for users and generative AI systems. Each answer is concise, factual and focused on business decisions.

ROI Measure the real impact of your data
Dashboards Turn numbers into better decisions
Deliverables Reports, KPIs, and actionable insights

A freelance data analyst helps businesses understand their data, measure performance and turn complex information into practical decisions.

They often work on short or one-off missions such as performance analysis, dashboard creation, data cleaning and KPI reporting.

The price depends on duration, complexity and the level of precision required.

For a short mission, you can typically start around $350 to $900 for 1 to 2 days of work, while a larger project spanning several weeks may range from $1,500 to $5,000 or more.

The most common tools are SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, Python and sometimes R.

Depending on the mission, they may also use PostgreSQL, DuckDB, Google Sheets, Airtable or warehouse tools to aggregate data before analysis.

The process usually starts with a scoping phase: defining the business problem, goals, available data and expected deliverables.

Then the analyst reviews the data, validates assumptions with the team and provides a dashboard, report or actionable recommendations.

A data analyst focuses mainly on descriptive analysis and translating data into operational decisions.

A data scientist usually works more on predictive modeling, machine learning and advanced algorithms, often for more complex use cases.

Security relies on clear rules: limited access to data, anonymization of sensitive files and a confidentiality commitment.

For sensitive projects, a processing protocol, precise access rights and strict export controls are strongly recommended, especially under GDPR rules.

Typical deliverables include a summary report, an interactive dashboard, KPI tables and action recommendations.

Depending on the project, you may also receive cleaned data files, a calculation model or segmented analysis by customer, channel or product.

A simple mission may last 1 to 2 weeks, while a larger project involving data collection, cleaning, analysis and dashboards may take 3 to 8 weeks.

Ongoing optimization or monitoring tasks are often handled on a recurring basis, such as 2 to 4 days per month.

ROI is calculated by comparing the value generated by a better decision with the cost of the mission.

For example, a dashboard that reduces losses by 5% on a $200,000 revenue base can create a potential gain of $10,000 while costing far less than the value it generates.

A freelancer often offers more flexibility, lower cost and faster communication.

This is especially useful for small businesses, startups or teams that need an expert on demand without the overhead of an agency.
