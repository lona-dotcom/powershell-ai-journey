E-COMMERCE

E-commerce dashboard: real-time sales performance

Challenge

Sales data was spread across several sources. The team tracked its metrics late and lacked visibility of the best-performing products and channels.

Solution

Data consolidation with SQL, metric preparation with Python and creation of a Tableau dashboard with filters for period, channel, product and geographical area.

Results

-30%reporting time
15%recoverable margin

A shared view of sales for commercial teams.

Tools and technologies

  • SQL
  • Python
  • Tableau
  • Excel
SAAS

Churn analysis: identify at-risk customers

Challenge

The cancellation rate was rising without a clearly identified cause. The company wanted to spot weak signals before customers left.

Solution

Exploratory analysis with Python, customer segmentation based on product usage, tenure and support interactions, followed by the creation of an interpretable risk score.

Results

22%at-risk accounts identified
-12%targeted churn

A prioritised action plan for the customer success team.

Tools and technologies

  • Python
  • pandas
  • SQL
  • Jupyter
B2B SERVICES

Challenge

Solution

Results

-18%duplicates detected
96%usable records

A reproducible quality control process.

  • Python
  • PostgreSQL
  • Excel
